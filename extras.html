Some other stuff I like

Fugglers, makeup, and batman are my top three, but they're not the only things I like. While there are TONS of Batman things to choose from, there are other characters (and one singular real person) I really like and some are kind of hard for me to find merch for. I'm also an avid press-on nail lover. More below!

Other Characters I like

Nightwing

I like Nightwing probably about as much as I like Batman, if not more. There just isn't as much merch I can find of him. Plus the only one I've seen that I really want is this hella expensive figure from mafex that I think got discontinued.

Cassandra Cain (Batgirl)

I think Cass is super cool and I love her suit design. I've also really been enjoying her current Batgirl run. Auramaster 9000 if you ask me.

Wonder Woman

Wonder Woman is truly underrated and tbh I haven't read much on her, but I want to. I think she's super cool. HOWEVER, please for the love of God DO NOT get me any Gal Gadot Wonder Woman stuff. They did her so dirty.

The Flash

I need to read more about the Flash to be honest, but I also think he's super cool. Bonus points if it's Wally West Flash! (Did you know that the Flash in the Animated Justice League show is actually Wally West, and not Barry Allen?)

Superman

I mean, come on, you can't NOT like this guy. Have you SEEN the new movie?? So so good, a great representation of Superman. Plus, he's Batman's best friend, they have the best friendship ever.

Joker

Honestly, I really only like Joker when he's being funny. A good example of it is the Lego verions of Joker. I did also think he's pretty sick in the Arkham games (Really any version of Mark Hamill Joker is great).

Starfire

The cutest ever!! She almost always has an amazing character design, it's hard to go wrong with her.

Raven

I wish I knew more about her, but she's always been my favourite titan.

Leon Kennedy (Resident Evil)

I got into Resident Evil earlier this year, I like what they've done with his character and how he's developed. (I also think he's attractive. Sue me.)

Claire Redfield (Resident Evil)

Claire is such an amazing protagonist. I was surprised by some of her actions in the game since I don't see a lot of strong female leads in games. Dare I say she's braver than Leon...

Rebecca Chambers (Resident Evil)

I really liked Rebecca in Resident Evil 0, I just think she has this cute charm to her and she's also a pretty good protagonist. Plus, her interactions with Billy in the game were so nice, I like their dynamic.

Billy Coen (Resident Evil)

I mostly just think he's cool, but I liked his story in Resident Evil 0. I think he had some depth and it had me feeling for him :(

Jill Valentine (Resident Evil)

Jill is another amazing female protagonist, I really enjoyed playing the RE3 remake (I played her in RE1 too but I got so lost). She's strong, brave, and I think she's really cool.

Carlos Oliveira (Resident Evil)

I liked that Carlos stuck to his morals when he realizes the truth about Umbrella. Also, he has charm and I think he's attractive. Sue me again.

Arthur Morgan (RDR2)

I bawled my eyes out when he died (straight sobbing, I'm not kidding) and actually felt like I had lost someone when I finished the game. He had so much depth, and I miss him like a real person. Sue me.

Cloud Strife (Final Fantasy 7)

I like that he's blunt (it's funny) and while he acts like he doesn't care, he's a good person and it shows in his actions. I also think he has major aura.

Zhongli (Genshin Impact)

I honestly don't even play this game anymore, not like I did five years ago, but I've always liked his design, plus I have nostalgia for this game. I still like him.

Park Jisung (NCT)

NOT a fictional character. He has a great voice and he's a great dancer. He is also great eye candy. Don't blame me. I'd enjoy a photocard of him but I could probably find one myself.

KNR Nails

If you ever see me with my nails done, they're almost always from K&R Nails. I tell everyone about them if they ask, that is how much I love this company. Plus, they're a Canadian small business! They also have a location at the Scarborough Town Centre.

Important Note

If you plan to buy any nails for me, I'm a size small! I also prefer shorter nails since I can't wear longer ones as often.
