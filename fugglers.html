Fugglers

I love these stupid looking idiots

At one point or another, you'd have probably heard me talk about these stupid dumb idiots. They come in all sorts of forms and for the most part, I don't care which kinds of fugglers you get me as long as they're either keychains or the normal 9-inch plushes (They have some standalone ones that are small collectibles, and I don't really have space to display right now). There are so many different kinds and I find them all stupidly funny and adorable.

HOWEVER.

There are TONS of places (COUGH COUGH MIND GAMES COUGH COUGH) that sell them way overpriced. I've seen keychains for $16 when the standard 9-inch plushes are about $18 (non special editions). For reference, I just bought a keychain that was about $12 (tbh tho I bought one from work and w my discount + price matching, I paid $10.50). DO NOT BUY OVERPRICED FUGGLERS, YOU ARE WASTING YOUR MONEY!!!

But fear not, there are a couple places I know of that do sell them for a reasonable price: The Real Canadian Supermarket has some for pretty average prices, and Walmart as well. Give those places a try if you ever want to get me a fuggler!
