Batman

Anything Batman-Themed is great!

You don't know me if you don't know the unhealthy obsession I have with this character. If you get me anything Batman related, I am happy. Say the wrong things and you'll find yourself in an unskippable cutscene where I explain a crapload of lore to you.

To be honest, because of my lack of space and money, I haven't been looking into that many specific Batman things I want. There are a few figures that I think were super cool but a lot of the really cool ones are like $300 which is HELLA expensive, plus again, I don't have a lot of space right now...

Important Note:

There are so many different versions of Batman: BTAS, Arkham, the several different live-action adaptions, and there are a few I don't like as much as others...

My favourite adaptations of Batman are: Batman: The Animated Series, The Batman Arkham game adaptations, the Robert Pattinson Batman, Absolute Batman (the one built like four fridges) and Lego Batman, of course.

The only version of Batman I don't like is the Zack Snyder Batman (Ben Affleck). The suit was lowk ugly and upon rewatching the Zack Snyder DC movies, he did a horrid job representhing these characters.
